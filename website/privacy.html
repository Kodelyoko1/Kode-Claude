Privacy Policy

Wholesale Omniverse LLC  ·  Effective date: August 21, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Wholesale Omniverse LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our VideoEditor / Aura Clipz service and its TikTok integration.

1. Information We Collect

TypeWhat we collectWhy
TikTok OAuth tokensAccess token and refresh token issued by TikTok after you authorize the appTo publish videos to your TikTok account on your behalf
Video filesVideo files you provide for processingTo apply enhancements and create short-form reels
Contact informationName and email address (if you subscribe to a paid plan)To manage your subscription and send receipts

We do not collect passwords, payment card numbers, or any TikTok account credentials (username/password). We only receive the OAuth access token TikTok issues after you authorize the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • TikTok tokens — stored locally on your device in your .env file; used solely to call the TikTok Content Posting API to upload and publish your videos.
  • Video files — processed locally on your device; the processed output may be uploaded to TikTok if you enable auto-posting. Files are not transmitted to our servers.
  • Contact information — used only for subscription management and support communications.

3. TikTok Data

Our integration with TikTok uses only the following scopes:

  • video.upload — to upload processed video files to TikTok's servers for publishing.
  • video.publish — to publish uploaded videos to your TikTok profile with AI-generated captions and hashtags.

We do not access your TikTok followers list, messages, analytics, or any data beyond what is required for video posting. TikTok data returned by their API is used only for the immediate operation and is not stored on any external server.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • TikTok OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device in a .env file and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Video files are processed locally; we do not store copies of your videos.
  • Subscription records (name, email, subscription status) are stored in a locally-managed JSON file.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • TikTok — video content and metadata you choose to post is transmitted to TikTok via their official API.
  • Payment processors — PayPal or Stripe may receive your name and email for billing purposes.
  • Legal compliance — if required by law.

6. Your Rights

  • Revoke TikTok access at any time: TikTok app settings → Privacy → Apps and Websites → remove Wholesale Omniverse.
  • Delete your local data: remove the TIKTOK_ACCESS_TOKEN and TIKTOK_REFRESH_TOKEN lines from your .env file.
  • Request data deletion: email WholesaleOmniverse@gmail.com and we will delete any subscription records we hold for you.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date.

01

Overview

This policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit or purchase from the Star Academy store at 1iyduv-11.myshopify.com, operated by Wholesale Omniverse LLC.

02

Information we collect

  • Contact & account information you provide at checkout or account creation: name, email, phone number, shipping and billing address.
  • Order information: items purchased, order value, order history.
  • Payment information: processed directly by Shopify's payment system (or another connected provider) — we do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Device & browsing information: collected via cookies, including pages viewed, cart activity, and general device/browser information.
03

Cookies & tracking

Essential cookies — Shopify sets cookies required for cart and checkout to function; these can't be disabled without breaking the ability to purchase.

Advertising cookies (Meta Pixel) — we use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel to measure ad performance and improve ad targeting. Opt out via your Meta ad preferences or your browser's tracking-protection settings.

04

How we use information

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Communicate about orders, shipping, and customer service
  • Measure and improve the performance of our advertising
  • Improve our store and product offering

We do not sell your personal information.

05

Sharing with third parties

  • Shopify — our e-commerce platform, which processes orders and payments on our behalf. See Shopify's privacy policy.
  • Payment processor — handles payment data directly; we don't store full payment details ourselves.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — when you complete a purchase, we send Meta a record of that conversion to measure ad performance. Your email and phone number are SHA-256 hashed before being sent — Meta never receives them in plain text. Order value, currency, the product(s) purchased, your IP address, and browser user-agent are sent unhashed as part of this event, used only for ad measurement and attribution.
06

Children’s privacy

This store is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

07

Data retention

We retain order and account information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, meet tax/accounting obligations, and support customer service, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

08

Your rights & choices

Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting WholesaleOmniverse@gmail.com. Opt out of Meta ad tracking through the Meta ad preference controls linked in Section 3.

09

Security

We rely on Shopify's PCI-compliant checkout for payment data and take reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we do.
